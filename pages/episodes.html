525–1220 CE · Ten episodes

Season One

Love, magic and mortality across seven centuries.

01
EPISODE 1 · 525 CE

The Curse Begins

As Ostangavia celebrates its annual Lantern Festival, Mel and Luca seem to have finally found the life they want together. But beyond the lights, Roman soldiers are disappearing, two children stumble onto something they were never meant to see, and Nyx and Seraphis are secretly gathering souls for a ritual connected to an ancient evil known as Moravok. As suspicion spreads and old family secrets surface, Mel is drawn into a confrontation that will transform her understanding of magic, love and mortality—and set in motion a struggle destined to reach far beyond a single lifetime.

02
EPISODE 2 · 573 CE

The Ka of Horus

Decades after tragedy changed Ostangavia forever, a mysterious traveller named Livana Sotmar infiltrates the town church and steals a hidden fragment of the legendary Book of Thoth. Her attempt to awaken the Ka of Horus during a lunar eclipse goes disastrously wrong, disturbing forces far beyond anything she intended and bringing unexpected changes to Mel’s world. With a dangerous magical text loose, Nyx searching for it and a mysterious young stranger stirring impossible feelings and memories, Mel finds herself racing against a thirteen-day deadline in the first true test of the curse that now governs her life.

03
EPISODE 3 · 666 CE

The Djinn’s Curse

A solar eclipse brings a new danger to Ostangavia when ancient Djinn and a resurrected general begin searching for a soul capable of anchoring them permanently to the mortal world. A seemingly ordinary drink becomes part of their plan, while Erik discovers an unexpected ability that could give the group their first real advantage. To stop the Djinn, Mel, Vitus, Greta and Erik must journey to the mysterious Hall of the Seven Winds, where seven trials guard an artifact powerful enough to contain what has been unleashed—but the growing chaos is also convincing some that magic itself may be the real enemy.

04
EPISODE 4 · 727 CE

The Iconoclast

Religious reform arrives in Ostangavia with Cassian of Cannstatt, an uncompromising official determined to bring sacred relics and supernatural practices under control. His arrival places Mel in the orbit of Father Lucian, a thoughtful priest and astronomer whose ideas about faith, knowledge and duty challenge her in unexpected ways. But when the attempted removal of an ancient relic tears open the boundary between the living world and Limbo, ideological arguments become dangerously real, forcing Mel and her companions to cross into a realm where the rules of life and death no longer apply.

05
EPISODE 5 · 777 CE

The Witch’s Fate

Fifty years later, the suspicion of magic that once simmered beneath Ostangavia has hardened into institutional power, and Mel’s investigation into a painful chapter of her past puts her directly in its path. Arrested and placed on trial for sorcery, she faces Elsbeth, a formidable prosecutor whose absolute belief in order makes her one of Mel’s most dangerous opponents yet. But as the proceedings expose uncomfortable truths on both sides, an extraordinary celestial event begins disturbing the familiar rules of Mel’s curse, turning a public trial into something far more personal than either woman understands.

06
EPISODE 6 · 800 CE

Dreamscape

The people of Tarningun are inexplicably falling sick, and the trail leads Mel to an apothecary who has retreated into a magical dream where grief, memory and reality can be rewritten. The mystery becomes uncomfortably personal when Mel discovers that the same kind of magic offers her something she has desperately wanted for centuries: a place where time cannot take the people she loves away. As the dream begins exacting a terrible price from the waking world, Mel must confront the seductive possibility that the most dangerous prison is one its prisoner does not want to leave.

07
EPISODE 7 · 850 CE

Monsters

When blood is spilled upon a forgotten burial mound, an ancient oath awakens with it, unleashing the draugr Sigvald the River-Cursed upon the living. As a supernatural infection begins spreading and transforming its victims, Mel discovers that brute force may be useless against an enemy created by centuries of dishonour and unresolved grief. With Nyx manipulating events from the shadows and Mel’s own reliance on the Dreamscape growing increasingly difficult to control, defeating the monster may require uncovering the human tragedy that created it.

08
EPISODE 8 · 925 CE

One Must Die

Another thirteen-day cycle begins with Ivalio’s return, yet for the first time Mel does not join the search. After more than a century of secretly using the Dreamscape artifact to escape into a world where Luca is always waiting for her, she begins withdrawing from the real world entirely—even abandoning the Lantern Festival she has loved since childhood. When Vitus, Ivalio, Greta and Erik discover that the artifact is no longer merely creating dreams but consuming Mel along with them, they face an impossible question: how do you rescue someone from the one place she desperately wants to remain?

09
EPISODE 9 · 1077 CE

Volumen ex Pineta di Classe

A mysterious fragment preserved in a remote monastery draws Mel and Vitus into the forgotten history of an ancient pagan grove, where a damaged sigil and a rare celestial alignment awaken a ritual left unfinished centuries earlier. The monastery becomes cut off from the outside world as past and present begin bleeding together, confronting them with a mysterious figure seemingly caught between different moments in time. Meanwhile in Ostangavia, Greta, Erik and Merdeha face a far less supernatural but equally dangerous opponent when a corrupt steward sets his sights on the village inn and the community built around it.

10
EPISODE 10 · 1220 CE

The Horde from the Steppe

A brilliant comet crosses the sky as the Mongol advance sweeps toward Europe, but something hidden within the approaching conflict has begun deliberately steering the invasion toward Ostangavia. When soldiers from a nearby outpost are taken hostage and the Oracle Forest itself begins to decay, Mel finds her usual sources of power slipping away just as Moravok’s influence reaches closer than ever before. Her search for another way leads deep beneath the forest and toward the unanswered mystery of her father’s disappearance centuries earlier, as the season’s final battle brings ancient magic, family history and the growing war with Moravok crashing together.
