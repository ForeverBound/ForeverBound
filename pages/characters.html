The people of Forever Bound

Characters

Main Cast

Ameldra "Mel" Runewood

A gifted witch and healer, Mel has spent centuries protecting Ostangavia while carrying a burden no one else can share. Wise, compassionate and fiercely determined, she refuses to surrender hope, even when fate itself seems determined to repeat the same tragedies again and again.

Luca della Forza

A courageous and compassionate leader, Luca is the heart of every generation he is born into. Though he has no memory of the lives that came before, his kindness, integrity and unwavering belief in doing what is right continue to inspire those around him in every age.

Father Vitus Benedictus

A Roman priest, scholar and spiritual guide, Father Vitus is the moral heart of the group. Calm, thoughtful and quietly humorous, he believes faith is something lived through action rather than words, offering wisdom and compassion even in humanity's darkest moments.

Greta

Analytical, disciplined and endlessly curious, Greta approaches every mystery as a problem waiting to be solved. Her passion for knowledge, careful planning and unwavering determination make her one of the team's greatest strategists, proving that understanding can be as powerful as magic itself.

Erik

Brave, impulsive and impossible to keep still, Erik throws himself headfirst into danger with a joke never far behind. His optimism and instinctive courage keep the group moving forward, even when everyone else is ready to give up, making him the heart that keeps hope alive.

Sylvara the Oracle

An ancient oracle bound to the sacred forest, Sylvara offers visions rather than answers. She reveals possibilities, consequences and forgotten truths, leaving others to decide which path they will follow.

Nyx

Once Mel's closest friend, Nyx becomes one of the greatest threats the world has ever faced. Powerful, intelligent and deeply wounded, she believes suffering can only be ended through control, placing her on a tragic collision course with the people she once loved.

Ivalio Santoro

A battle-hardened veteran haunted by past mistakes, Ivalio struggles to balance guilt with responsibility. Gruff but deeply loyal, he becomes one of Mel's most trusted allies, proving that redemption is earned through the choices we continue to make.

Merdeha

Loud, filthy and impossible to ignore, Merdeha makes her living collecting the town's waste while secretly knowing more about its people than anyone else. Beneath the outrageous jokes and relentless flirting lies one of Ostangavia's oldest survivors—a cunning keeper of secrets who has learned that information is often more valuable than gold.

Moravok

Born from forbidden knowledge, Moravok is an ancient force that feeds on corruption, fear and imbalance. Patient and manipulative, he prefers to reshape people from within, making him an enemy whose influence can be far more dangerous than open warfare.

Recurring Characters

Capra Lactaria

No ordinary goat, Capra Lactaria has guarded the Runewood family for generations. Quietly intelligent and mysteriously perceptive, this unlikely companion often senses danger long before anyone else and has a habit of leading people exactly where they need to be.

Caelum Roric

A loyal soldier and dependable friend, Caelum rises through duty rather than ambition. Practical, honourable and steady under pressure, he provides the quiet leadership that helps hold Ostangavia together during its darkest hours.

Mary Baker

Warm, welcoming and known throughout the town for her famous bread, Mary Baker reminds everyone that history is shaped not only by heroes, but by ordinary people who choose kindness, community and resilience in difficult times.

Master Bertram

Known simply as the cemetery keeper, Bertram has quietly tended Ostangavia's graves for as long as anyone can remember. A lonely and unsettling figure, he avoids company and rarely speaks, spending each night caring for the town's dead. Most people choose not to ask where he came from—or why he never seems to leave.

Seraphis Santoro

Brilliant, ambitious and endlessly searching for certainty, Seraphis believes the world can only be saved through absolute order. His pursuit of control leads him down an increasingly dangerous path, forcing those closest to him to question how far good intentions can be taken.
