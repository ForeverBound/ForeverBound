A historical fantasy musical

Forever Bound

What if your soulmate was reborn every few centuries—but never remembered you?

Across fifteen centuries of history, Mel, an immortal witch, is forced to watch the man she loves live, die and be reborn again and again. Each lifetime offers another chance to find him, but ancient enemies, forgotten prophecies and a mysterious curse conspire to keep them apart.

From the final days of the Roman Empire to the Mongol invasions and beyond, journey through haunted forests, lost civilizations and timeless myths in an epic fantasy musical where history and legend collide. Alongside her companions, Mel faces ancient mysteries and impossible choices as she searches for a way to break the cycle.

As magic fades from the world and a shadow born from forbidden knowledge threatens to unravel reality itself, their struggle becomes greater than the curse. They must fight to preserve the stories, people and places that make humanity worth saving. Blending mythology, mystery and emotional drama, Forever Bound explores love, memory and sacrifice—and the price of fighting fate itself.
