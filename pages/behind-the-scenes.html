Behind the scenes

From one Halloween song to an entire world

Forever Bound began in October 2024 when a friend asked for a funny Halloween song to perform at a talent night.

What was meant to be a one-off joke quickly became something much bigger. Over the following weeks, one question kept leading to another: Who was the person singing the song? Where did she live? Who was she singing about? Why did magic exist in her world?

As those questions accumulated, characters gained histories, places gained names, and the joke gradually disappeared. What remained became Forever Bound — a story stretching across centuries, with its own mythology, history, characters, music and world.

A fictional city in a real history

Building Ostangavia

The story begins in 525 CE in the fictional city of Ostangavia, placed in the region around modern-day Tübingen in southern Germany. The fantasy is invented, but the geography and historical world around it are deliberately grounded in real places and periods.

Historical maps, settlement patterns, surviving landscapes and period research help shape the world. As the story moves through different centuries, the politics, religions, architecture and conflicts surrounding the characters change with it.

Research becomes story

History is part of the plot

Real historical events are not simply scenery. They are used as pressure points that change what the characters can do, what people believe, and what dangers the supernatural story can exploit.

727 CE

The Iconoclast

Byzantine iconoclasm and Emperor Leo III's campaign against sacred images become the catalyst for the episode's conflict over relics, faith and magical power.

777 CE

The Witch's Fate

Changing religious authority and institutional persecution shape Mel's arrest and trial, turning historical ideology into a direct threat to the characters.

850 CE

Monsters

Viking activity, ancestry and burial tradition feed directly into the supernatural story of an old blood oath and the draugr it awakens.

Making the world

From research to 3D

Characters, sets and environments are built in 3D, with Blender at the centre of the visual production process. Historical references are translated into architecture, interiors, landscapes, props and character environments before being developed into the final stylised look of the series.

Music develops alongside the story rather than being added afterwards. Songs can begin as character ideas, emotional beats or pieces of mythology and then evolve together with the scenes they belong to.

On the road

Location shots used for inspiration

Research for Forever Bound has also gone beyond books and maps. Trips to places including Tübingen, Trier and Cochem have been used to study ruins, landscapes, historic streets, fortifications, churches and surviving architecture.

These places are visual references rather than direct copies. Details from different locations and periods are combined to create a world that feels rooted in real history while still belonging completely to Forever Bound.

The location archive will continue to grow as research trips add new reference material.
